01 · Personality

Luxury you can experience, not just purchase.

JewelryBox speaks quietly and precisely. It never shouts a discount. It describes weight, light, and the moment a piece is first worn.

Voice: assured, tactile, editorial. Short sentences. Nouns over adjectives. The customer is addressed as a collector, not a shopper.

06 · Art direction

macro · watch crown, black bg
ring on skin · single hard light
necklace on glass · reflection
product · ivory sweep, soft shadow

Catalogue shots — 4:5, product centred at 70% width, ivory (light) or obsidian (dark) sweep, one soft shadow falling bottom-right. No drop shadows added in post.

Editorial shots — 3:4 or 16:9, single hard key light, deep blacks, controlled specular highlights. Skin and fabric allowed; faces cropped or turned.

Macro — 1:1, fill the frame with one detail: bezel teeth, prong, clasp, pavé. Focus falls off within the frame.

Moissanite — shot on black with a moving light source so fire reads as colour, never on white.

Never — lifestyle stock, coloured gels, busy props, more than one piece per frame unless a set.

07 · Motion

Slow, deliberate, expensive.

ease: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) — every transition. Nothing bounces.

durations: 240 / 600 / 1200ms — hover · reveal · hero.

Reveals: 24px rise + fade, staggered 80ms per line. Images scale 1.04 → 1 over 1.2s.

Page change: 400ms fade to bg, content enters from 16px below. Cart: slide from right, 600ms, backdrop 40%.

Loading: JB monogram, hairline draws around it once every 2s.
